# to_do_catchem

1. Who is the audience for this app
    - The intended audience for this app is mostly for people who are fond of Pokemon. This app is a Pokedex and was specifically made for anyone who likes Pokemon and would find it fun to just go around collecting them. I took a lot of inspiration from Pokemon Go, but my app's general gameplay, or intended gameplay if I ever come back and implement it, is more of a clicking game with RNG. 

2. What the app does
    - Right now, in its current state, the app just has a list of the starter Pokemon from generations 1-3. There are more generations, but they are commented out for the sake of not having to scroll so much. At the bottom of the screen, there are three buttons. One red, one green, and one blue. These buttons take you to different zones, Fire, Grass, and Water. In these zones, Pokémon with their respective typing show up, and you have a certain amount of time to click on them and catch them as they bounce around the screen. The zones work, but the Pokémon are the only things that haven't been added, so for now it is mainly just a very small Pokedex with floating action buttons that take you to the different catching zones.

3. Why the app is useful
    - Honestly, I have no idea how this app is useful. I personally thought it would be fun to make a little Pokémon catching game that isn't like Pokémon Go, since in Pokémon Go you have to walk around in the real world to actually have different types of pokemon spawn in, but this app is supposed to just be something that you sit down, relax, and catch some Pokémon.
